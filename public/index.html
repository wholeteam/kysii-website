Hey, I'm Kysii.

Husband, dad, true hip-hop head, tech junkie and human “search engine.” Newport News, Virginia raised, Morehouse educated, Atlanta made.

Tune into any of my shows and you'll find out quick: I'm a down-to-earth guy, the one family and friends call when they need a resource or some advice. I believe in karma and inner peace, I try to live stress-free, and I love putting a spotlight on my guests as we take on the topics everybody's talking about.

Long before podcasts were everywhere, I was working with unsigned independent artists, building a one-stop shop that helped hundreds of them market and distribute their music. That grew into the X Squad Affiliates — a network of podcasters and broadcasters — and then X Squad Radio.

Today my wife Angela and I own Cafe Bartique on Cascade Road in Atlanta, and by trade I'm an identity and privileged-access security engineer with Generational Technology Group. My short-form content is where all of it comes together.
